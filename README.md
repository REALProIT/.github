# **REALProIT – Industrial Digitalization for Real 🚀**

**We build the industry of the future by connecting machines, systems, and people – from sensor to boardroom.**

## **About Us**

REALProIT are specialists in industrial IT and digitalization with a focus on **MES**, **UNS**, and **system integration**. We are pioneers in delivering services tailored for production environments, navigating the complexities of Industry 4.0 with ease.

We help Swedish industry become more data-driven, secure, and competitive by bridging the gap between Operational Technology (OT) and IT.

## **Our Expertise**

We work at the intersection where code meets real-world production. Our primary focus areas are:

* **Unified Namespace (UNS):** We design architectures for a "single source of truth" on the factory floor, reducing technical debt and making data accessible for AI and analytics.  
* **MES & Production Systems:** We build and manage systems for traceability, real-time insights, and optimization.  
* **Integration Services:** We create robust modules to connect everything from scales and PLCs to ERP systems via modern protocols like MQTT and REST.  
* **OT Security:** We build secure solutions that protect production without hindering the flow of data.

## **Tech Stack & Methodology**

We believe in clean code, measurable results, and modern tools.

* **Languages & Frameworks:** .NET (C\#), SQL, MQTT/Sparkplug B.  
* **Infrastructure:** Docker, Containers, Cloud & On-prem solutions.  
* **DevOps:** We practice what we preach with CI/CD, automated testing, and code reviews.  
* **Agile:** We deliver in small, secure steps with clear milestones.
